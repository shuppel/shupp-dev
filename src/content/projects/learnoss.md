## LearnOSS

A world-class education is already sitting on the open web — students at great
universities publish their lecture notes, professors give away entire textbooks,
and whole courses live in public repos. What's missing is compilation: structure,
context, and connection.

LearnOSS pulls those pieces together into free, structured learning resources:

- **Compiled notes** — openly published lecture notes organized into study units,
  with original summaries and key ideas, every lecture linking back to its source
- **Linked data** — each course embedded in a web of open resources: official
  course sites, sibling courses at other universities, and reference wikis
- **Free books & visuals** — free-first book references and original SVG diagrams
  for the concepts that only click when you can see them

The first course is [CS 111 Operating Systems Principles
(Stanford)](/learnoss/csc111-stanford), compiled from Aditya Saligrama's openly
published lecture notes — 21 lectures organized into ten study units covering
threads, scheduling, virtual memory, file systems, crash recovery, and virtual
machines.

Attribution is the whole game: LearnOSS links and summarizes, never copies, and
the original notes stay canonical.
