Ephemeral explores temporary interfaces shaped by a person's current need.

Try the interactive study at [/design/ephemeral/](/design/ephemeral/). Compare a conventional feed with three ways of using the same fictional posts: catching up, understanding a topic, and browsing. Explicit intent overrides the sample time of day. Preferences change the content, and original posts remain inspectable.

The study uses authored responses and deterministic rules. No model inference, live social data, or behavioral tracking is involved. Saved posts last for the current page visit, including after the temporary view is dismissed.
